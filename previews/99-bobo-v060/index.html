About this comparison

Generated locally on an Apple M5 Max with 128 GiB memory, using H3 Apple v0.6.0 and the original LightX2V four-step recipe. Each scene was generated once in each mode, with no retries or seed selection.

Measurements, review scope and generation records

Times cover complete generation for these six runs. The queue ran serially, alongside brief release-page work and CPU video processing. These are whole-mode examples; they do not establish component-level speed gains or a general quality ranking.

Visual review sampled each original at 6 fps and was not blind. The paper boat partly leaves the bottom of the VSA frame; SOL has some soft edges during the boat contact. Both modes stylize the glowing insect, especially VSA’s larger, butterfly-like wings. Voice quality, exact dialogue and precise lip sync have not been certified.

Exact prompts, settings, timings, file hashes and review notes (JSON). Source and model identities are recorded there; the reference images are identified by hashes and are not included in this gallery.

Original videos retain their complete frames and audio. Comparison videos add mode labels and re-encode the picture, with two original audio tracks copied without mixing. VSA is the default track; use the individual originals for reliable audio selection in your browser.
