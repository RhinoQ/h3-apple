(S1) One continuous eye-level shot outside a small bakery on a quiet morning. A woman in a green apron opens the glass door, carries a chalkboard menu onto the pavement, sets it down carefully, and straightens it with both hands. She smiles toward the camera and says, "Fresh bread is ready." Warm light falls across the bread in the window; a bicycle slowly passes behind her. Natural footsteps, a soft door chime and steady street ambience. No cuts, no music, no subtitles.
