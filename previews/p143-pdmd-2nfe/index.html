Can two steps replace four?

Five paired Ref2VA examples: the released 0.6.0 LightX2V recipe on the left, an experimental PDMD two-step transfer on the right. Each pair uses the same reference pixels, prompt and initial noise. Play both together, select either original audio track, or seek to the same moment.

Not adopted as the 0.6.0 default. Across four validation pairs, complete generation is 1.761× faster at the median (2.041× for denoising). The two-step candidate has new prop-continuity failures in the long scene, recurrent grid-like breakup, and reference leakage. The released product keeps its four-step recipe.

The videos below are the complete, unchanged MP4 originals with generated stereo audio. This is a non-blind comparison. Subjective listening and human blind evaluation were not performed.
