Measured on a Mac

Apple M5 Max, 40 GPU cores, 128 GiB unified memory, macOS 26.6.1; PyTorch 2.11.0 FP32 MPS, existing VOSR2 one-step.

Time includes imports, asset verification, detection, tracking, model loading, restoration, compositing, diagnostic frames, export and delivery checks. Environment/model setup and separate comparison exports are excluded. Memory is the one-second sampled sum of process-tree physical footprints; GiB = 2³⁰ bytes. No RSS fallback was needed.

The source clips took 1,032.8 seconds to generate in their recorded production runs. Full-film enhancement adds 40.4% of that effort. This is a budget comparison, not a generator speedup. The measured optional-processing time has been accepted for this preview.

Method, checks and all attempts

Existing VOSR2 weights, FP32 MPS, one step per 512-pixel face canvas, a stabilized 256-pixel source crop and fixed seed. No new source video was generated for this repair. The restorer processes individual images; it has no temporal conditioning.

An inherited eye-distance eligibility rule initially missed most of the side profile. Removing that irrelevant alignment rule and bridging only isolated, unambiguous one-frame detection gaps corrected coverage. The profile scene became calibration; the different-character scene was frozen before its output was seen.
