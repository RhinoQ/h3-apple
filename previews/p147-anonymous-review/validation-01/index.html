Methods in this set
A · Native resolution + standard VAE
The unchanged v0.6.0 baseline: native-resolution generation and standard H3 video VAE decoding. Original prepared references.
L · Low resolution + Lanczos
Generate at approximately half width and height, decode with the standard H3 video VAE, then enlarge with fixed Lanczos. Original prepared references.
C (= X) · Low resolution + X2 VAE
The frozen validation candidate is X: the same low-resolution latent as L, floating-point X2 decoding, original prepared references. B32, decoder quantization and streaming optimization are off.

Same delivered size, duration and original audio format within each group. L and X/C share the same final low-resolution latent and PCM. Other generation trajectories can produce different audio and motion.

Group 03 · Two-character key handoff

3 samples · 5 seconds each

Original task prompt
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is the pink rabbit with long ears and yellow dress in <Picture 1>. The three views describe one rabbit.
<Subject 2> is the brown bear with green shirt and small brown bag in <Picture 2>. The three views describe one bear.

summary:
A five-second clean hand-drawn animation of two friends carefully handing over one small brass key.

retention_analysis:
[Shot 1] Retain the identities, clothing, black outlines and pastel coloring of <Subject 1> and <Subject 2>. Place them in a simple cream-colored room at a blue tabletop.

detailed_description:
[Shot 1] Static waist-up two-shot with the rabbit at left and the bear at right; both faces and their hands are visible above the table. The rabbit holds one small golden brass key by its round bow, with a narrow shaft and two rectangular teeth clearly visible. The rabbit extends the key toward the bear. The bear takes the bow with its right paw; after contact the rabbit releases and withdraws its empty paw. The bear raises the same key slightly and smiles at the rabbit. End with the one key in the bear's paw and the rabbit's paw visibly empty. Keep the little key and its outline readable during contact and release. No captions or writing.

overall_soundscape:
Soft hand movement, one light metallic tick at the transfer and quiet room tone. No voice.

Group 05 · Lettering, thin lines and occlusion

3 samples · 5 seconds each

Original task prompt
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is the white thin navy-outlined card with the exact navy word SYNC and three fine horizontal navy lines in <Picture 1>.
<Subject 2> is the single solid coral vertical bar at the left of the card.

summary:
A five-second flat graphic animation in which a moving foreground bar briefly occludes a labeled card, followed by a small card tilt and a clear final lockup.

retention_analysis:
[Shot 1] Retain the pale blue background, one white card, its navy border, the four SYNC letters, its three fine lines and one coral bar. The bar is in front of the card.

detailed_description:
[Shot 1] Static camera on the composition in <Picture 1>. The coral bar slides steadily from left to right across the front of the white card. It covers the letters and the three lines as it crosses; each stroke reappears unchanged behind the passing bar. After the bar reaches the right side, the whole card tilts slightly clockwise and returns level. Its lettering and three lines stay attached to the card. Hold the unchanged word SYNC and all three separate horizontal lines clearly visible for the final second. No additional words or decorative elements.

overall_soundscape:
A soft sliding swish follows the bar, and one short dry click marks the card returning level. No voice.

non_diegetic_music:
None.

Group 07 · Three-shot compass handoff

3 samples · 15 seconds each

Original task prompt
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is Naruto in <Picture 1>, the blond young adult ninja with blue eyes, whisker-like cheek marks, metal forehead protector and orange-and-black jacket.
<Subject 2> is Sasuke in <Picture 2>, the dark-haired young adult ninja with pale skin and gray open-collar shirt.

summary:
A fifteen-second live-action-style three-shot scene. Naruto gives Sasuke one small brass compass; Sasuke checks it, then both leave together.

retention_analysis:
[Shot 1] Retain both subjects' identities, hairstyles, clothing and realistic appearance. Set them on a quiet stone path in the village shown behind the references.
[Shot 2] Retain the same subjects, positions and the single brass compass transferred in Shot 1.
[Shot 3] Retain the same subjects and compass ownership established in Shot 2. Sasuke still holds it as both leave.

detailed_description:
[Shot 1] At 00:00.000, eye-level medium two-shot. Naruto stands on the left facing Sasuke on the right. Naruto shows one small round brass compass in his open right palm, says, "Take this," and passes it to Sasuke's left hand. Show contact, Naruto's release and Sasuke holding the same compass. Naruto's right palm is then empty.
[Shot 2] At 00:05.000, cut to a closer view from the same side, showing Sasuke's face and the compass in his left hand, with Naruto visible at the left edge. Sasuke tilts the compass once; its glass face, dark needle and circular brass rim remain intact. He looks from the compass to Naruto, nods and says, "I know the way."
[Shot 3] At 00:10.000, cut back to a wider eye-level two-shot. Sasuke lowers his left hand while still holding the compass. He turns toward the path and begins walking away. Naruto turns and walks beside him. End with both distinct characters moving down the path, the compass still with Sasuke. Keep the scene free of subtitles or overlaid writing.

overall_soundscape:
Consistent quiet outdoor wind, subtle fabric movement, a small metallic touch at the transfer and footsteps as they leave. Naruto speaks the first English line; Sasuke answers with the second. The two young adult male voices are distinct and synchronized with the visible speaker.

non_diegetic_music:
None.

Track the compass through the transfer, inspection and departure. Record whether Sasuke still holds it as they leave, or whether this cannot be determined.
