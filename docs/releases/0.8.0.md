# 0.8.0 — More detail, on your Mac

**Generate a five-second video with sound in about three minutes. Then give your
best takes a sharper finish.**

SOL + X2 remains the fast default. New VOSR super-resolution doubles native
video dimensions and preserves the original audio.

[Install](../install.md) · [Watch the videos](https://rhinoq.github.io/h3-apple/#showcase)

## What's new

- **One-command super-resolution:** `h3 upscale --input native.mp4 --output detailed.mp4`.
  Native 768p becomes 2732×1536; portrait and native 576p are also supported.
- **Less waiting for detail:** the five-second **Celestial Hall** clip took
  **20m 11s** to restore, down from **46m 30s** with FP32 — **56.6% less time**.
  **Stairway Above the Clouds** took **20m 03s**.
- **A complete Mac workflow:** automatic model preparation, CLI and Python,
  progress, cancellation, input checks and reproducible records.

The two showcases use **native 768p + VOSR 2×**. Their roughly 20-minute
restoration time is additional to generation. Both passed frame checks and a
complete, method-disclosed human playback review; local fine-texture differences
remain. [Measurements and review scope](../evidence/v0.8.0-vosr.json).

## Fast generation stays simple

**3m 14s median** for a five-second video with SOL + 544p/X2, measured across
12 scenes in the 0.7.0 benchmark on a 40-core M5 Max / 128 GiB. This covers the
complete generation call after model setup. VSA remains available with `--mode VSA`.

[Compare all 48 benchmark outputs](https://rhinoq.github.io/h3-apple/#results),
including failures, prompts and references. The benchmark retains its original
build, scores and timing data.

## What we built

We made full-video VOSR restoration practical in the same Mac CLI and Python API:
faster processing, repeatable results, original audio and automatic model setup.

| Foundation | Our contribution |
| --- | --- |
| [VOSR](https://github.com/cswry/VOSR), Qwen-Image VAE and DINOv2 models and inference code | Apple GPU integration, video processing, audio preservation and model verification. |
| PyTorch's native Apple GPU kernels | Selected FP16 compute while preserving sensitive FP32 operations; fused normalization, prepared weights and reduced tile-submission stalls. |
| Existing SOL, VSA and X2 community work | One product workflow with the established model-preparation, duplicate-person and colored-grid fixes. [Code and idea attribution](../development.md#implementation-and-credits). |

The models and GPU kernels come from their credited authors. Our contribution
is the Mac adaptation, precision and execution policy, product integration and
verification. [Full notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES).

## Get started

Use the [installation guide](../install.md). Existing compatible generation models
are reusable. VOSR requires **128 GiB memory**, 20 GiB free working space and a
separate **6.97 GB** model download. Its inherited restoration components include
noncommercial terms. [Guide and license details](../upscale.md).

[Validation](../validation.md) records installed-package tests and media checks.
