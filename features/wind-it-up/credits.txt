Wind It Up · h3-apple 0.9.0

The three video shots and their stereo tracks use the six-step Pika H3 Flex Ref2VA adapter in h3-apple High on an Apple M5 Max. The input images were made for the film with OpenAI ImageGen. The close-up of the toy and the close-up of the real car are two panels extracted from the same original continuity board. The aerial reference was generated using the real-car panel as an appearance reference. The art directions are included in reference-art-direction.txt.

Creative references: Pika's lip-gloss commercial uses macro photography and tactile action sounds. Its station dance assigns separate jobs to setting and identity references. The film, its images and its prompts are original.
