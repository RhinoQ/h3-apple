# Compare outputs

Use this guidance for authorized reviews. Record builds, weights, inputs,
settings and measurements. A successful case supports a candidate, not a
universal quality claim.

## Define the comparison

Complete-release comparisons measure combined behavior. To attribute an effect
to one component, hold other conditions fixed or report confounders: reference
preprocessing, weights, adapter identity/scale, schedule, arithmetic and
postprocessing. A matching prompt and seed are insufficient.

Keep frozen prompts unchanged; register revisions as separate interventions.
Reuse a saved control only when inputs and execution are compatible. One exact
replay proves agreement only for that checked case.

## Inspect the action

Review the full progression and consecutive frames around defects. Compare both
elapsed time and event phase when timing differs. Verify the requested action
and resulting state: cleaner frames caused by omitted, delayed or reduced motion
do not establish better motion quality.

Use frame progression to distinguish duplicate contours from blur, occlusion and
stylization. View at delivery size; use crops to resolve uncertainty. An isolated
pose can locate a defect without showing its effect in playback.

## Bound the conclusion

Report action, identity, temporal artifacts, speech, text and speed separately
when relevant. State reviewed intervals, methods and whether identities were
hidden. Partial relief leaves a remaining defect; unreviewed parts stay unverified.

Byte or decoded-media equality proves preservation, not creative success.
Choose delivery candidates using the user's priorities. Retain failures and
uncertainty rather than retroactively counting them as passes.
