P139 affine_native.py adapts the quant_f16_u8_row_g64 and affine_group_sums_w8g64
arithmetic from the pinned vpipe source:
https://github.com/tgo-app-dev/vpipe/blob/f34e2cc3a3adae759eea254419f436f5b7800057/gpu-kernels/metal/quant/affine_dequant.metal

The shared gemm_u8q_impl excerpt in p138_affine.metal is specialized with
UB_MMA=true, CMODE=1, SPLIT=false, STRIP=8 and BF16 tensor operands.
The adopted final store writes BF16 directly. An FP32 store followed by BF16
conversion remains available only for the independent numerical oracle.
This has no BF16 dequantized-weight residual and no original-output accuracy claim.
Scalar pointer address-space adaptation is inherited from P138.
