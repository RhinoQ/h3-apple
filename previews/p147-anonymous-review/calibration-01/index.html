Methods in this set
A · Native resolution + standard VAE
The unchanged v0.6.0 baseline: native-resolution generation and standard H3 video VAE decoding. Original prepared references.
L · Low resolution + Lanczos
Generate at approximately half width and height, decode with the standard H3 video VAE, then enlarge with fixed Lanczos. Original prepared references.
X · Low resolution + X2 VAE
Decode the same final low-resolution latent used by L with the floating-point X2 VAE. Original prepared references; no B32, decoder quantization or streaming optimization.
E · E34 reference reconstruction + X2
Reconstruct each prepared reference with E34, resize it back to its exact original prepared dimensions, then use the same RGB for Qwen and the standard reference VAE. Low-resolution generation and floating-point X2 output; no B32 residual.
D · E34 + B32 detail enhancer + X2
Add the B32 detail residual to E34 reference reconstruction at strength 1.0, resize back to the fixed prepared-reference dimensions, and pass the same enhanced RGB to Qwen and the standard reference VAE. Low-resolution generation and floating-point X2 output.
A′ · Wider native-canvas control
Calibration-only standard-VAE control: generate at 1408 × 768 and center-crop to 1366 × 768, compared with A’s 1376 × 768 canvas. No X2 or B32.

Same delivered size, duration and original audio format within each group. L and X/C share the same final low-resolution latent and PCM. Other generation trajectories can produce different audio and motion.

Thin dark outlines and letter strokes stay continuous through the perspective change. Hold the front-facing PLAY card and disk at right for the last second. All motion is smooth and readable.
